## 🧭 Foundational Principle (RFC-0012)

The CypherGuide protocol provides decentralized technological solutions (Bitcoin Lightning / Cashu payments, Nostr cryptographic signatures, portable reputation). However, **paying via Bitcoin/Lightning DOES NOT CHANGE and DOES NOT EXEMPT a host from any legal obligations under local national sovereignty** (residency registration, fire safety, public order licensing, income taxes).

## 🇻🇳 Vietnam

- **Contributor:** CypherGuide Community / Nostr Vietnam
- **Last Updated:** March 2026
- **Referenced Regulations:** Decree 282/2025/NĐ-CP (effective Dec 15, 2025, superseding Decree 144/2021/NĐ-CP), Law on Entry, Exit, Transit, and Residence of Foreigners in Vietnam.

### 1. Temporary Residence Declaration for Foreign Guests
- **Timeline:** Overnight foreign guests must be registered with local ward/commune police within **12 hours** (or up to **24 hours** in remote areas) via the Immigration Department's electronic portal or directly in person.
- **Enforcement Risk:** Failure to declare temporary residence of foreign guests carries an administrative penalty of **3,000,000 VND to 5,000,000 VND** (under Decree 282/2025/NĐ-CP).

### 2. Business Registration & Tax
- Hosts operating regular commercial lodging establishments or exceeding regulatory income thresholds must register a household business and declare personal income tax / VAT per local tax department guidelines.
- Receiving Sats/Bitcoin over Lightning Network is a peer-to-peer technical payment agreement; it does not eliminate the legal obligation to declare lodging activities conducted on physical property in the territory.

### 3. Public Order & Fire Safety Compliance
- For dedicated commercial lodging operations, properties must satisfy applicable fire safety standards and hold certificates of compliance regarding public security and order issued by competent police authorities.
